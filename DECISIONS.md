# Decisions

One line per choice the brief left open.

- **AI provider:** Claude through the official Anthropic SDK (`claude-opus-5-5`, low effort) or Gemini over REST (`AI_PROVIDER=gemini`, default `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`: on the free tier `gemini-flash-latest` timed out at 40 s, `gemini-2.5-flash` is retired for new users, and `gemini-3-flash-preview` took 22 s per call). Key and model come from `.env`.
- **Model export:** the gradient-boosting model is exported from scikit-learn to JSON (trees, isotonic table, conformal threshold) and evaluated in TypeScript, instead of ONNX. No native runtime to install; a self-test vector in the file checks Python/TypeScript parity at load.
- **Feature parity:** the training cohort is generated and featurised by the same TypeScript code the app uses (`npm run cohort`), and Python only trains and evaluates on the exported CSV, so training and serving features cannot drift.
- **Hybrid verdict policy:** the model can only make a file more cautious. Agree: that verdict. Opposite confident verdicts: not sure. Rules confident, model unsure: rules verdict plus a disagreement notice. Rules unsure, model confident: not sure plus a notice.
- **History rules:** both "under 6 months: -1" (rule table) and "under 12 months: -1" (corner cases) apply, so 6 months of history costs 1 point and under 6 costs 2.
- **Jahan Fish Trader** gets 12 months of history (section 8 table) rather than under a year (corner-case list), because with the "under 12 months: -1" rule a seasonal trader could never reach viable on the rule table's maximum of +3. Its seasonal pattern is still owner says, the band is widened and the file asks for last year's months.
- **Seasonality:** business type is used only to pick a seasonal profile (fish trade, knitwear); the profile is used for trend adjustment and the watchman when the type is seasonal or the owner says seasonal.
- **Festival spikes** are damped by winsorising the season-adjusted series at 1.6 times its median before trends are measured.
- **Karim Pharmacy commitments:** Karim is not sure at filing but carries seeded commitments made through the partner's pooled facility when the manager approved with conditions. The listing rule (viable verdict and approve decision) applies to new listings, so Karim is not open for new commitments.
- **Investor share:** investors may fund up to 25% of a loan through the partner's pool; "amount open" is that share minus commitments.
- **Demo investor:** `investor@demo.bd` is verified and holds commitments; `newinvestor@demo.bd` is unverified so the verification wizard and the commit block can be shown.
- **Watchman timeline:** history runs Nov 2025 to Oct 2026; "now" is Nov 2026; Simulate next month reveals up to 6 post-funding months.
- **Watchman interval:** 80% interval from empirical quantiles of one-step-ahead ratios, minimum half-width 12% (plus 6% when the seasonal profile is borrowed from the business type).
- **Missed check-ins** produce a "no data" row, never distress; two missed months in a row escalate to a call. The statement is still compared with the forecast.
- **Fixed-threshold baseline** for the watchman: alarm when a month falls more than 30% below the previous month. Shown beside each forecast for comparison.
- **Counterfactual search** tries every subset of up to 3 actionable changes and keeps the cheapest that moves the verdict up; when none can, it returns the cheapest change that moves the rule total closest to the next verdict, marked partial.
- **Phrasing:** template text by default (fast, deterministic); "Write with AI" runs the AI cross-check and phrasing with the validator. `generated_by` is `ai_phrased` or `template`; the verdict source (rules only or model hybrid) is stored separately in `verdict_source`.
- **Bill images:** the seeded bills are rendered in the browser from templates (Noto Sans Bengali on a canvas), so the AI reader receives a real PNG. Uploaded images are resized in the browser and never stored on the server.
- **Duplicate check** keys on date, direction, amount, party and running balance: split walk-in sales are not duplicates; a copied row is.
- **Auth:** JWT in an httpOnly cookie, also accepted as a bearer token so a cross-domain deployment can work without third-party cookies.
- **Rules-only band:** centre from a logistic map of the rule total, base half-width 8 points, capped at 95%.
- **Honesty label:** at the team's request the full-width striped ribbon became a slim animated gold line plus a small "Demo data" badge in every header (with a tooltip carrying the full sentence), and the landing page lost its limits box. Investor pages keep the SIMULATION banner and the printed file keeps the full synthetic-data line.
- **v2 brand config:** name and tagline live in one file, `brand.config.json` at the repo root, read by the server at runtime and imported by the client. The brief copies in the repo were updated to the v2 tagline so no old tagline remains anywhere.
- **v2 roles:** logins are lender@demo.bd (policy owner), investor@demo.bd and newinvestor@demo.bd. The shop owner has no login: they apply at the public /apply link and follow their file at /owner/<token>. The lender sees every AI decision read-only and never approves or declines a case. Karim's funded loan is seeded as an existing loan in the partner's book before Bhorsha (its filing decision is a hold), which is how a not-sure file can still be watched after funding.
- **v2 online evidence formats:** courier settlement CSV = order_id, date, amount_collected, fee, net_remitted, settlement_date (one row per delivered COD order; couriers pay out in weekly batches); order log CSV = order_id, date, channel, amount, status. These are our assumption of a typical Bangladeshi courier export and are not confirmed with any courier. Courier payouts land in the wallet as unclassified credits and only count as sales once matched.
- **v2 matching rules:** a bill is checked only when ONE wallet debit matches amount (within 1%), date (within 7 days of the bill or paid date) and supplier label (Bangla and English compared through a consonant sound skeleton). Courier rows are matched per payout batch (rows sharing a settlement date, summed net of fees and partial remittances) to ONE courier-labelled wallet credit within 0 to 10 days; when several credits fall inside the 1% window, only an exact, unique amount counts. Anything else stays owner says and becomes a Bangla owner question.
- **Materiality:** an unmatched item is material when its amount exceeds 10% of average monthly verified sales (last 3 months). Only material items block an approval.
- **Pending payouts:** a courier batch that settles within 10 days of the statement end is "pending", neither checked nor a question.
- **Receivable invoices** (Sultana) cannot match a debit; they count as checked when the named buyer paid the shop through the wallet in at least 3 different months. The amount owed is still what the invoice says.
- **Verified courier sales are dated by delivery**, not by the weekly payout date, for sales features; balance and consistency checks still run on the raw wallet rows. Couriers and prepaid online customers are channels, not buyers, so they are excluded from customer concentration.
- **Robust trend:** the 6-month trend uses the Theil-Sen slope (median of pairwise slopes) on the season- and festival-adjusted series; festival adjustment applies to the online share of hybrid and digital-first sellers.
- **Demo fixture tuning:** Rafi's synthetic order stream (4.5 orders a day, about 600 BDT each, 3% monthly growth) was chosen so the demo shows a growing hybrid seller; this tunes a demo fixture, not the evaluation cohort.
- **v2 agents:** the pipeline runs as five named agents (Intake, Verifier, Underwriter, Watchman, Servicer) inside one case run; each step is written to the audit log with the agent name, a run id (`<case>:v<verdict version>`) and its timing, and the case page shows the latest run as the agent trace. Steps 4 and 5 are committed together because the Underwriter agent and the decision logic are one piece of code.
- **"Model disagreement" for the fail-safe gate** means the model's conformal set does NOT contain the rules verdict (a contradiction). A set holding both outcomes means the model is less certain: it is shown as a notice but does not block. This keeps the weak classifier from either approving or silently vetoing everything.
- **Decision default is a hold.** The Underwriter records approved_with_conditions only when every gate passes; declined_with_plan only when the rules say not viable and the evidence itself is sound; otherwise hold_need_evidence. Over the autonomy cap the first tranche is capped and the rest is tied to conditions and on-time instalments.
- **Every pipeline run records a new decision**; decision history is never edited. A policy change applies only to new decisions (settings no longer recompute old files).
- **Supplier invoices condition:** when a shop has supplier payments in the wallet but no supplier bill uploaded, the file carries the condition "Provide supplier invoices for stock".
- **v2 owner questions and answers:** an answer is stored as owner says (it never becomes checked) and closes the question, which satisfies the material-evidence gate; resubmitting a fresh statement, courier CSV or order log replaces the earlier upload of that kind, and every resubmission records a new decision while keeping the earlier ones.
- **v2 circuit breaker:** two limits from the policy: the hold rate among the most recent AI decisions (window and minimum count configurable) and the simulated default rate (share of funded demo loans with an open call-level flag). It is checked after every decision, every simulated period and every policy save; tripping pauses AI decisions with the reason recorded, and only the lender resumes. While paused, new runs are recorded as hold_need_evidence citing the pause.
- **v2 watchman cadence:** a day-based simulation clock (Nov 2026 to May 2027). Hybrid and digital-first shops are checked every week, cash-led shops at each month end. "Simulate next week" advances 7 days; "Simulate next month" advances to the first of the next month, running every check that falls due. After funding, the watchman counts courier payouts from couriers verified at filing as sales (the partner would receive the courier feed in servicing).
- **Weekly band:** weekly sales are noisier than monthly, so weekly checks flag against the 5th percentile of one-step-ahead ratios (monthly checks use the 10th). Festival weeks are divided out by the expected lift on the online share; the first 10 days after a festival get a 30% wider downside.
- **Servicer offer:** at call level the Servicer offers a 14-day repayment-date shift, our assumed policy allowance (simulated); the owner can accept it on their page. Investor updates go out only for watch or call flags, never for no-data or seasonal lulls.
- **AI time limits:** check-in reads time out after 8 seconds and fall back (labelled), so a slow AI response cannot stall Simulate next month.
- **v2 · Band shape at the limits.** The likelihood band is shifted, not cut, when it touches 2% or 95%, so its width always shows the uncertainty (a low-coverage or seasonal widening is never hidden by clipping). The calibrated probability is capped to 2% to 97%: a 292-shop calibration set cannot support "100% likely".
- **v2 · Step 11.** `data/olist/` and `data/berka/` are absent, so both stay "planned" on /evaluation with N = 26 weeks fixed in the protocol; nothing was downloaded.
- **v2 · Segment trade-off statement.** The brief expected cash-led shops to be held more often. The synthetic evaluation measured the opposite (healthy approval: cash-led 23%, hybrid 0%, digital-first 9%), mostly because unmatched courier payouts wait for the owner's answer. README, About and /evaluation state the measured direction, not the expected one.
- **v2 · Wording.** No manager or officer anywhere in the app. Annotating a month is logged as `lender:annotate_month` and never changes a decision.
- **v3 loan agents:** five more agents (Contract reader, Counter-offer, Pricing, Fee explainer, Payment matcher) join the pipeline, and the Servicer and Watchman gain repayment duties. Each follows the same pattern: the AI reads, proposes or writes; the code in `lib/loanTerms.ts` validates, clamps and computes; a labelled fallback runs without a key. The seed uses the fallbacks so `check:seed` is deterministic.
- **Limits and rates:** loans up to Tk 1,000,000 and 12 months; borrower rate 14%; investor rate = bank rate 10% + 0.25%; Bhorsha keeps 8% (tenor up to 3 months), 8.5% (4 to 6) or 9% (7 to 12) of the interest. A policy save is refused when the borrower rate is below the investor rate plus Bhorsha's fee (fee in rate points = fee share x borrower rate) at any fee tier.
- **Re-termed demo loans:** every demo loan moved to 12 months and the amounts were scaled so each shop keeps its instalment-to-margin ratio (the only feature that depends on loan size), so every earlier seed expectation still holds. Sultana's sales were raised so a Tk 600,000 loan still crosses the autonomy cap and shows tranches. Two shops were added: Mitu (unclear late clause, held until the lender confirms) and Hasan (Tk 1,500,000 over 24 months, counter-offer).
- **New gates:** `within_limits`, `rate_setup` and `contract_terms`. A request outside the limits is `declined_with_plan` with a counter-offer; an unclear or failing contract is a hold. The counter-offer is the largest amount (Tk 10,000 steps) inside the limits that the rules still judge viable.
- **Annex pricing:** the standard contract states the rebate and penalty ranges and leaves the figure to Annex A, which the Pricing agent fills; a contract that fixes its own figure wins over the agent, and both are clamped to the lender's bands.
- **Payments:** a signed webhook (HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body). A quoted loan reference matches with certainty; otherwise the AI weighs payer, amount and timing, with a code score as fallback. Below the confidence threshold (default 0.8) the payment goes to the lender's review queue and is not applied. Payments apply to the oldest open instalment; early means more than the grace days (3) before the due date, late means more than the grace days after it.
- **Repayment watch:** an early, late or partial payment goes to the Watchman. A real warning routes to the lender's watchlist and the Servicer sends a late notice; a seasonal pattern is only noted on the loan.
- **Lender institutions:** every lender account is an institution with its own cases (applications made through its own `/apply/<code>` link), versioned policy, AI pause and circuit breaker, simulation clock, loans and repayment review queue. Every `/cases/:id` route checks ownership and answers 404 otherwise. Investors see the approved listings of every institution, labelled with the lender. A start-up migration (`ensureTenancy`, idempotent) moves data from the earlier single workspace to the demo lender and gives every lender an application code, so the live database needs no reseed.
- **Admin:** a read-only `/admin` overview of all institutions and investors. The account comes only from `ADMIN_EMAIL` and `ADMIN_PASSWORD`; public sign-up can create lenders and investors, never admins.
- **Release funds:** after an AI approval the lender releases the first tranche (or the whole loan) through the licensed partner (simulated); that opens the repayment account. The watchman skips periods with no new wallet data, so a newly released loan is not flagged for "zero sales".
- **Application limits:** the form shows the chosen lender's limits and warns, in plain words, when a request is above them; such requests still go through and get a counter-offer. The form refuses only nonsense amounts (above Tk 50,00,000 or 60 months).
- **Investor returns:** listings, the commit box and the portfolio show the investor rate (bank rate + spread of the loan's lender), the interest earned and the total coming back over the loan's tenor, as an amortising schedule on the investor's amount. "Received so far" passes on the investor's share of each borrower instalment paid. These are contract rates if borrowers repay in full, labelled as not promised. Listings and portfolio refresh every 20 seconds.
- **Early-payment discount (lender policy):** an instalment paid in full at least `early_pay_min_days` (default 5) before its due date has `early_pay_discount_pct` (default 25%, 0 = off) of its interest waived at the point of repayment. This is separate from the early-settlement rebate on repaying the whole loan.
- **Annex ranges are clear by rule:** the AI contract reader sometimes flagged the standard "set in Annex A (between x and y)" clauses as unclear, which held healthy applications at random. The code now drops such flags; vague clauses without a range are still held.
- **Investor funding share by loan size (replaces the flat 25% and the personal caps):** investors choose freely how much to put in, with no per-investor limit; the only limit is what a loan still has open. Investors together may fund up to 100% of a loan of Tk 5,00,000 or less and up to 75% of a larger one, so the lender keeps at least a quarter of every big loan. All three numbers are lender policy settings (a lender that wants skin in every loan can set the small-loan share below 100%). The open check is one atomic update, so two investors cannot overfill a loan.
- **Investor identity by NID scan (replaces the dummy identity box):** the AI reads the front of the NID (name in English and Bangla, parents, date of birth, number) and flags visible editing; the code decides: a Bangladesh NID card, read with at least 70% confidence, a 10/13/17-digit number (17-digit numbers must start with the birth year), age 18 or over, the name matches the account, no signs of editing, and the NID not used by another account (an HMAC fingerprint of the number, never the number itself). The investor confirms "this is me". Kept: name, date of birth, parents, last 4 digits, the fingerprint; the image is never stored. If the reader is down, typed details get the same checks and are labelled as typed. These are consistency checks, not proof a card is genuine: that needs the Election Commission's NID verification through the licensed partner (next step for a real launch, shown as such). The NID photo is the one model call that cannot be redacted, since reading it is the purpose; it is purpose-limited and not retained. The demo uses SPECIMEN cards from the test kit, labelled as such.
